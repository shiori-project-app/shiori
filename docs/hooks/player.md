# Hooks: player

- `useVideoPlayer(videoRef, startAt)` returns `{ time, player }`. `player` holds the `<video>` state (duration, playing, waiting, ended, rate, error) and controls, and stays the same object while only `time` moves. Volume and mute are saved as `player-volume`, and `timeRef` and `durationRef` are available to handlers. Seeks go through `createSeekCoalescer`.
- `usePlaybackMilestones({ episodeId, player, time, onStarted, onWatched })` fires `onStarted` once and `onWatched` at 90% or at the end. It saves resume progress every 15 s while playing, on pause and on unmount, but never before the duration is known.
- `usePlayOnOpen(play)` starts playback when the route was opened with `PLAY_ON_OPEN` state (`constants/routes.js`), then clears that state so Back doesn't restart it. Episodes otherwise open paused.
- `usePauseWhenHiddenToTray(videoRef, pause)` pauses when the window closes to the tray, unless the video is in picture-in-picture.
- `usePlayerHotkeys(player)` adds keyboard shortcuts and ignores keys that belong to focused controls. Space or k toggles playback, j and l seek ±10 s, left and right seek ±5 s, up and down change volume, m mutes, c toggles subtitles, s skips a segment, f toggles fullscreen, t toggles theater mode, p toggles picture-in-picture, Home and End jump to the start and end, and 0–9 jump to tenths of the video.
- `useAudioTracks(videoRef)` lists and switches audio tracks, and remembers the preferred language (`player-audio-language`).
- `useSubtitles(file, videoRef, overlayRef)` loads embedded subtitles through `getSubtitles` and picks the default from the saved preference (`player-subtitles`). It renders ASS with the ASS renderer and plain cues as a text track. `toggle`, used by the hotkey, brings back the preferred track. Files without subtitles leave the preference alone.
- `useSkipSegments({ malId, episode, duration, time, playing, seek, auto })` provides AniSkip intro and ending segments, the current segment, the skip action, auto-skip, the "skipped" notice, and `isPrompting` and `promptRemaining` from `useSkipPrompt`.
- `useSkipPrompt(segment, time)` returns `{ isPrompting, promptRemaining }` for the skip button, using `nextSkipPrompt` and `skipPromptProgress` from `skipTimes.js`. `isPrompting` is true for the first `SKIP_PROMPT_SECONDS` of video time after entering a segment (less if the segment ends sooner), counted again after seeking back more than a second, and `promptRemaining` is the share of that time left, from 1 down to 0. Both follow video time, so they stop while paused or buffering.
- `usePictureInPicture(videoRef)` returns `{ isSupported, isActive, toggle }`. It leaves fullscreen first, and closes picture-in-picture when the player unmounts.
- `useFullscreen(elementRef)` returns `{ isFullscreen, toggleFullscreen }`. It puts the whole app window in fullscreen and the player fills it (`is-fullscreen`), so fullscreen survives the player being replaced for the next episode, and it makes everything outside `elementRef` inert while fullscreen. The exported `exitFullscreen()` runs when `WatchPage` unmounts and when `PlayerCrashMessage` or `EpisodeUnavailable` appears, so losing the player always leaves fullscreen.
- `useInertOutside(elementRef, isActive)` makes everything outside the element inert while active, so Tab and keys can't reach the page hidden behind the fullscreen player. Live regions such as toasts and the offline banner stay as they are, so screen readers still announce them. It restores only what it changed.
- `useIdle(ms)` returns `[isIdle, markActive]`, used for hiding controls. It counts from when it mounts as well as from each `markActive`, so a player opened without touching the mouse still goes idle.
